# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The user explicitly requested an HTML page.

## Users

Wedding guests opening a directly shared invitation link, primarily on mobile, to enjoy the invitation, find event details, and prepare a reply.

## Product Purpose

Create a memorable wedding invitation that makes celebration details easy to find and gives guests a clear RSVP path.

## Positioning

One champagne-gold and off-white invitation unfolds through native continuous scrolling, with small optional moments of discovery.

## Operating Context

Guests open a message link on phones or laptops. The current user instruction is “just build a demo ill give details later.” Exact event details remain intentionally provisional where not supplied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive static template with six sections: welcome; invitation and three photographs; scratch date, calendar actions and countdown; full proposed agenda; automatic Avenra drawing and directions; RSVP.
- Continuous native scrolling, not page flips or gated reading.
- Confirmed: Naveen and Dulanjani, 4 December 2026, Avenra Gardens Hotel in Negombo, Sri Lanka.
- Unconfirmed: ceremony time, final agenda, real couple photographs, and RSVP delivery contact. Sample images and proposed programme are visibly disclosed.
- Google Calendar and iCalendar save an all-day event. The countdown targets midnight at the start of the confirmed date in Sri Lanka, not an invented ceremony time.
- No backend: RSVP prepares an unsent, copyable message and does not store responses. An optional guest URL parameter prefills the name.

## Brand Commitments

Champagne gold and off-white are the user's visual commitment. The latest pinned video references guide the continuous invitation. The current composition is an implemented demo, not a separately approved comp.

## Product Principles

- Make essential wedding information easy to find through native scrolling.
- Let optional motion enrich the invitation without gating content.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from replaceable demo content.
- Make the venue recognizable and directions easy to open.
- Preserve reduced-motion, keyboard, and small-screen behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic sections, native validation, keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and descriptive image labels. All six sections and the date remain readable without JavaScript. External maps/calendar destinations need a connection. Physical iOS/Android and Safari testing have not been performed.
