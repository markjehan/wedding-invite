Help

Tap a question to see what to do. Nothing here can break the invitation, and almost everything can be put back.

Every day

How do I send someone their invitation?
  1. Find them on Guests, or under What to do next on Home.
  2. Press the green Send button. WhatsApp opens with the message and their link already written.
  3. Check it, then press send in WhatsApp. Come back to this page when you are done.

The list marks them as sent as soon as you press Send here.

How do I know if someone has replied?

Type their name in Find a guest on Home. Next to their name it says in words where they are: not sent yet, sent, opened, coming, or can't come.

How do I remind people who haven't answered?

On Home, under Remind them, press Remind next to a name. WhatsApp opens with a gentle reminder and their link. To see everyone still to answer, open Waiting for a reply.

How many people are coming, for the venue or the caterer?

The big number on Home is the number of people coming. Each side splits it between the bride's and groom's sides.

For the full list, press Download the list as Excel. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets and has everyone, their seats and their answer.

When something goes wrong

A guest phoned or told me in person

Open their name, then under Did they reply another way? choose how many are coming and press Save: coming, or press Save: can't come.

A guest pressed the wrong answer, or changed their mind

They can open their own link again and change it themselves{% if replies_close %} until {{ replies_close|date:"j F" }}{% endif %}.

Or you can do it for them: open their name and use Change their reply. To let them answer again from scratch, press Clear their reply.

They say the link doesn't open, or they can't find it
  1. Open their name and press See what they see. If their invitation opens for you, the link is fine.
  2. Send it again: open their name and press Send the invitation again. It is there even after they have replied. Or press Copy link and paste it into an SMS or email.
  3. Ask them to tap the link rather than typing it. If they are using an old phone, try opening it in Chrome.
They need more seats than we kept

Open their name, change Seats kept for them under Their details, and press Save changes. Their invitation lets them choose up to that many straight away.

I sent it to the wrong number
  1. Open their name, fix the WhatsApp number, and press Save changes.
  2. Press Mark as not sent if it shows, then press Send again.

Anyone who got it by mistake only sees that guest's invitation. If you would rather they had nothing, take the guest off the list and add them again: they get a brand new link and the old one stops working.

It says “Sent” but I closed WhatsApp without sending

Open their name and press Mark as not sent. They go back under Send their invitation.

The Send button isn't there, it says “Add their number”

That guest has no WhatsApp number yet. Press Add their number, type it any way you like (077 123 4567 is fine), and press Save changes.

Someone is on the list twice

Open the one that has not replied and press Take them off the list at the bottom. If both have replied, keep the newer reply.

I can't find someone I'm sure is on the list

Type just part of the name, like “Nim” for Nimal, or the last four digits of their number. Check Both sides is chosen, and press Show everyone.

Signing in

Where does the 6-digit code come from?

From the authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar). Open it and type the 6 numbers shown for this guest list. They change every 30 seconds, so use the newest one.

I lost my phone or changed phones

Use one of your backup codes instead of the 6-digit code. Each works once. If you have none left, ask the person who made your account to reset it.

Once you are in, Sign-in codes and backup codes lets you set up the new phone and see fresh backup codes.

Is it safe to use on someone else's computer?

Yes, if you press Sign out at the top when you are done. The pages are never saved on the computer.
