How a reply reaches you

  1. The guest presses “Send my reply”. Their answer is saved here straight away, so Home always has it.
  2. WhatsApp opens on their phone with the reply already written, addressed to the number for their side.
  3. They press send, and it arrives as an ordinary WhatsApp message.

A guest's side is set on their page, or in the Side column of your Excel list.

Changing a number here changes it for every reply sent after you save.
